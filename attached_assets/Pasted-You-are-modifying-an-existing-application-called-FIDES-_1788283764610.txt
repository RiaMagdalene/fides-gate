==================================================
PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================================================

FIDES GATE is NOT a generic cybersecurity dashboard.

FIDES GATE is a verification and accountability gateway that sits between publishers and AI crawlers.

Its core purpose is:

1. Verify WHO the crawler is.
2. Determine WHAT the crawler claims it wants.
3. Apply an access policy based on VERIFIED identity + declared intent.
4. Provide the appropriate content payload.
5. Add forensic signals where appropriate.
6. Record suspicious/violating activity in a public accountability ledger.

The central security principle is:

"Never trust crawler intent without verified crawler identity."

The key attack that FIDES GATE demonstrates is:

A crawler claims:
X-Purpose: rag

but has no valid registered Ed25519 signature.

FIDES GATE must NOT trust the claim.

It must automatically apply the strictest policy and create a ledger event.

This attack-and-detection flow is the central story of the product.
